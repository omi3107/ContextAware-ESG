# ContextAware-ESG: Context-Aware ESG Comparability

## Overview

ContextAware-ESG is a research project focused on determining whether Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) figures can be fairly and reliably compared across companies, reporting frameworks, and time periods.

ESG figures may look similar but have different meanings, definitions, reporting boundaries, measurement methods, or time periods. Comparing them without considering these differences can lead to misleading conclusions.

This research focuses on understanding the context behind ESG figures before deciding whether they can be fairly compared.

## Research Objectives

- Assess ESG figure comparability across different companies.
- Identify differences in definitions, units, boundaries, and measurement methods.
- Examine ESG disclosures across different reporting frameworks.
- Track changes in ESG figures and disclosures over time.
- Identify missing, inconsistent, or redefined disclosures.
- Explain why a comparison is valid, invalid, or uncertain.

## Comparison Scenarios

The research considers four primary comparison scenarios:

1. **Different Companies – Different Industries:** Comparing ESG figures between companies from different industries.
2. **Different Companies – Same Industry:** Comparing ESG figures between companies in the same industry.
3. **Same Company – Different Frameworks:** Comparing disclosures under frameworks such as GRI, ISSB, ESRS, and BRSR.
4. **Same Company – Different Years:** Examining changes in ESG figures and disclosures over time.

## Core Research Principle

**Don't compare numbers first. Compare what the numbers mean first.**

A comparison should only be considered reliable when the underlying concepts and reporting contexts are sufficiently aligned.

Missing or unavailable information should remain unknown rather than being treated as positive or negative performance.

## Current Research Scope

The current research focuses on environmental indicators, including:

- Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions
- Energy consumption
- Water consumption and withdrawal
- Waste generation and management

Social and Governance dimensions are part of the broader research context but are outside the current evaluation scope.

## Expected Outcome

The research aims to develop a system that can assess ESG figure comparability and provide:

- A comparability verdict.
- Reasons supporting the verdict.
- Important contextual differences.
- Evidence supporting the assessment.
- Disclosure gaps and historical inconsistencies.
- Explanations of uncertainty when information is insufficient.

## Research Vision

To make ESG comparisons more transparent, meaningful, and reliable by evaluating what figures actually represent rather than relying on numerical similarity alone.
